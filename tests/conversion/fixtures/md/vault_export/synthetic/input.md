# A Study of the Commons

The commons is older than the market[^1] and has outlived several of them.[^2]

Networks of this kind are vast and open (Harvey [2020](David%20Harvey%20-%20Anti-Capitalist%20Politics.md "Harvey, David. 2020. Anti-Capitalist Politics. London: Pluto Press."); Beck [1992](#^beck1992); Cox [1981](https://doi.org/10.1177/03058298810100020501 "Cox, Robert W. 1981. Social Forces, States and World Orders (Beyond International Relations Theory). Millennium 10(2).")).

The point is made at length elsewhere.[^3]
