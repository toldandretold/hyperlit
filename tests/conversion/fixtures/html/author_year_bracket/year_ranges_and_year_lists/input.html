Spans, lists and the years in between

Serrano's first term (2014-2019) was quiet; the second term (2019-2024) was not. During the earlier administration (2009-2014) the pattern already held.

The programme was defended repeatedly (Serrano, 2024a, 2024b; Okonjo, 2024). Serrano framed it as a mandate (Baptiste, 2024; Serrano, 2019, 2023).

The cumulative-advantage argument (Halloran 1968, 1988) still applies, and the survey (Okonjo, 2018: 1761-1764) reports the same.
