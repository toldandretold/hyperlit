# Two different things get called "AI crawlers" and they deserve opposite answers:
#   1. TRAINING crawlers hoover the corpus into a model's weights. Blocked.
#      The texts here are the commons; they are not raw material.
#   2. RETRIEVAL crawlers fetch a page because a person asked a question right
#      now, and answer them with a citation and a link back. Allowed.
# Blocking (2) does not protect the commons — it just means nobody using an
# assistant to find open-access research is ever told this place exists.

# works if they are current, so check before adding to either list.
